## About this policy

This policy explains how opus55video (opus55video.net) handles your information. Contact us at [support@opus55video.net](mailto:support@opus55video.net) with any questions.

## Information we process

- **Account data:** your name, email address, and sign-in session information. If you sign in with a third-party account such as Google, we receive the basic profile details it shares.
- **Creation data:** the prompts, images you upload, generation settings, task status, and the resulting videos, so you can find your work in your creation history.
- **Billing data:** orders, subscriptions, and credit balance. Payments are handled by our payment provider (currently PayPal); we do not receive or store your full card or bank details.
- **Support and usage data:** messages you send to support, plus basic technical and analytics data about how the site is used.

## How we use it

We use this information to run your account, generate videos, charge and refund credits, process payments, provide support, prevent abuse, and improve the service. To generate a video, your prompt, settings, and any uploaded images are sent to third-party AI model providers, which process them to return the result. Do not include sensitive personal information in a prompt or image.

## Cookies

We use cookies needed for sign-in and to remember your language, and analytics cookies to understand site usage.

## Retention and your choices

We keep account, creation, and billing records while your account is active and as required by law, such as for tax and accounting. To access or delete your information, or to close your account, contact [support@opus55video.net](mailto:support@opus55video.net). Requests are handled under applicable law.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date above shows the latest revision.
