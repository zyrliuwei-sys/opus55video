## About this policy

opus55video is a cutting-edge AI video generation platform at opus55video.net, where you can experience the latest trending AI video generation features. Contact us at [support@opus55video.net](mailto:support@opus55video.net) with questions about this policy.

## Guest video generation

You can open the video studio without creating an account. We set a 30-day, HTTP-only guest cookie so you can see your recent video tasks in the same browser. We store a hash of that guest identifier, a hash of the requesting IP address, your prompt and video settings, the task status, and the result video URL. The IP hash helps enforce daily usage limits. Clearing the cookie ends access to that browser's task history, although server records may remain until deleted.

We send your prompt and generation settings to Fal to create the video. Fal processes that information and hosts the generated result under its own privacy terms. Do not include sensitive personal information in a prompt unless you are comfortable sending it to that provider. Provider-hosted links may expire under Fal's retention rules.

If you choose to create an account for other site features, we process account and session information needed for sign-in. If you contact support, we process the message and any attachments you choose to provide. Optional payment, analytics, email, or storage integrations may process information when those features are enabled and used.

## Retention and requests

We retain task and operational records while needed to provide the service and meet legal requirements. For questions or deletion requests, contact [support@opus55video.net](mailto:support@opus55video.net). Requests are handled under applicable law.

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date above shows the latest revision.
